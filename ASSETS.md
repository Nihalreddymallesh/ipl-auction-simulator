# Assets

All graphics in this project are **original, locally generated SVGs** — no copyrighted
IPL franchise logos or player photographs are bundled.

Official IPL team logos are trademarks of their respective franchises and were
**intentionally not downloaded**. The generated crests use publicly known team
color associations with original shield artwork. Users can replace any logo via
**upload or URL** in Team Setup.

## Player images (`public/assets/players/`)

94 player avatars (`<player-id>.svg`) are generated by the same script:
role-colored gradient backgrounds, player initials, role and country text.
Player photographs were **not downloaded** (licensing/restrictions); every
player therefore has a clean, working local avatar and no broken images ever render.

A second fallback layer exists in code (`src/components/PlayerImage.tsx`,
`TeamLogo.tsx`): if an image fails to load or a custom entry has no image,
a styled initials block is rendered instead.
